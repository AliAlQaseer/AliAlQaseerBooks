In the name of Allah the Merciful

Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds

O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad

Abdul Salam Al-Harawi narrates that: I heard Abal Hassan Ali son of Musa
Al-Ridha (peace be upon him) saying:

“May Allah have mercy upon the servant who revives our matter. I asked
him: And how does a person revive your matter? He said: “He learns our
knowledge and then teaches the people, indeed, if people knew the
beauties of our speech, they would follow us”.

The holy Prophet (peace be upon him & his family) has said:

"A person would not be a believer unless I become the most beloved from
himself and my family would be the most beloved from his family and my
progeny would be the most beloved from his progeny and Myself would be
the most beloved from his-self".
